# Instructions for AI agents (Codex, Claude Code and others)

You are one of several agents working with a human team on a web app prototype for a plant-related hackathon. **Read `docs/CONTEXT.md` first**, then the role file for your role in `docs/agents/` (`STREAM_A.md` data and intelligence, `STREAM_B.md` pages and visuals, `CODEX.md` deliverables and tests, `INTEGRATOR.md` merging). The human will tell you your role. If you were not told, ask.

A single paste-able briefing for your role is generated by `tools/agent_brief.sh <a|b|codex|integrator> <case-id>` into `docs/briefs/<role>.md`.

## Essentials (the full rules are in `docs/CONTEXT.md` and `CLAUDE.md`)
- Single-file web app built by `python3 build.py [case-id]` from `src/` and a case pack in `cases/<id>/`. Do not edit generated files (`dist/`, `knowledge-hub-integrated_BAP5.html`, the zip) and never commit them.
- Setup: `bash tools/setup_env.sh`; `export CHROMIUM=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium`. Use `python3.13` if `python3` lacks openpyxl, pillow or python-pptx.
- Tests: `npm run test:quick`, `node tests/smoke_case.js <built html>`, `node tests/layout.js --quick --strict`.
- Work only in the files your role owns. Shared files (`build.py`, `src/base.html`, `tests/regress.js`, `tests/smoke_case.js`) belong to the integrator.
- Your own branch (`a/...`, `b/...`, `codex/...`), small commits, push often, never force-push, never push to `main`. The integrator merges into `integration`.
- Coordinate only through `docs/TASKS.md` (sessions cannot see each other). Update your lines; add a handoff note when you finish something.
- Text rules: plain engineer wording, no em or en dashes, no emoji, `%` not "percent", units after a space, no placeholders, no invented people or plant data, synthetic data labelled.
- The assistant in the app must never explain how to bypass a trip or interlock; hazard results stay labelled screening estimates.
- Netlify: `docs/NETLIFY_MULTI_CASE.md`. Workflow: `docs/WORKFLOW.md`.
