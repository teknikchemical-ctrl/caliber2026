## Optimisation and economic metrics selected for development
1. **Discrete lead-demand loss functions.** From a nonnegative weighted distribution compute P(demand > stock), expected unfilled units and expected surplus. These are conditional model quantities, not historical stockout measurements.
2. **Critical-fractile optimisation.** Minimise overage cost x expected surplus plus underage cost x expected shortage. The empirical optimum is a distribution quantile at underage/(underage + overage). [Larry Snyder's Stockpyl newsvendor implementation](https://github.com/LarrySnyder/stockpyl/blob/master/src/stockpyl/newsvendor.py) documents this equation and its units. We will evaluate the empirical distribution directly rather than assume normal demand for lumpy spares. Underage consequences are Not in baseline data, so any financial optimum requires an explicit caller-supplied penalty. Never equate replacement price with plant downtime loss.
3. **Tail shortfall metrics.** Report the quantile and mean of the worst 5% of modelled unfilled quantities, integrating fractional probability at the tail boundary. [Riskfolio-Lib's CVaR_Hist documentation](https://github.com/dcajasn/Riskfolio-Lib/blob/master/riskfolio/src/RiskFunctions.py) provides the conditional-tail formula. Our application is to nonnegative material shortage, not financial returns. This is a mathematical diagnostic, not calibrated plant-loss risk; do not sum per-part tail risks as a joint portfolio tail.
4. **ROP diagnostics before caps.** Separate high service target, skewed demand, dated campaign uplift and lead-proxy variation. Publish dynamic-to-workbook ratios by demand class. An arbitrary cap can hide the very rare demand a critical spare protects; any cap must be an explicit reviewed policy scenario, never a silent correction.

## Evidence gates and excluded methods
Historical stock/receipt balances, open orders, actual stockouts, installed-equipment failure distributions, repair loops, substitution and verified downtime costs are absent. Therefore multi-echelon repairable-spares models, equipment-level reliability/availability optimisation, causal downtime ROI and claimed emergency-price savings are not supported. They can be assessed only after the relevant source data is supplied.

The 13/4-month chronological holdout is conditional on identical snapshot initial stock. It is more useful than in-sample fit for demand generalisation, but is not a historical inventory reconstruction. Preserve service and stock/funding metrics together. S/A are never auto-released; insurance remains. Never add capital release twice, add consumption expense to purchase cash, or add economic carrying indicators to cash NPV.
